Privacy policy

Effective September 28, 2026

The short version. Gravity Garden has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. The game itself keeps your progress only on your device. The game is free and shows ads, and the advertising partners that serve those ads collect some device and usage data, described below. On iPhone and iPad they can only track you across other companies' apps if you allow it.

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Gravity Garden (the “game”) on iOS, iPadOS or Android, why, and the choices you have. “We” means the developer of Gravity Garden.

1. Information the game keeps on your device

The game saves the following locally on your device so it can remember your garden between sessions:

  • your best score and tutorial progress,
  • your Dew balance and the waters, pipes and other items you own or have equipped,
  • your settings (music, sound effects, haptics, tilt controls) and whether you bought “Remove Ads”.

This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you delete the game. We do not run our own servers and do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or any other personal information.

2. In-app purchases

You can buy Dew packs and “Remove Ads”. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details; the store only tells the game which item was bought so it can deliver it. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies apply to those payments.

3. Advertising

The game shows ads between runs and optional rewarded videos (for example to revive or double your Dew). Ads are delivered through the Appodeal mediation platform, which selects an ad from partner ad networks. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent ad fraud, Appodeal and these partners automatically collect:

Advertising partners

Depending on your region and which network wins an ad, data may be received by partners that include Google AdMob and Ad Manager, AppLovin, Unity Ads, ironSource / LevelPlay, Meta Audience Network, Mintegral, Liftoff / Vungle, Pangle, InMobi, BidMachine, Bidon, DT Exchange, Amazon Publisher Services, Bigo Ads, Chartboost, Moloco, MobileFuse, myTarget, Ogury, PubMatic, Smaato, Verve and Yandex (Android only), plus the measurement services Adjust, AppsFlyer, Firebase and Sentry that Appodeal uses for attribution and crash reporting. Each partner handles data under its own privacy policy. Appodeal keeps an up-to-date list of its partners in its privacy policy.

4. Your choices

  • Tracking on iPhone and iPad. The first time ads load, iOS asks whether Gravity Garden may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, your advertising ID is not shared and ads are not personalised based on it. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads on some devices).
  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland. Where required by law, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. If you decline, you may still see non-personalised ads.
  • No ads at all. Buying “Remove Ads” stops interstitial ads. Rewarded videos stay optional and only play when you tap them.
  • Deleting data. Deleting the game removes everything it stored on your device. Because we hold no data that identifies you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. For data held by an advertising partner, you can reset your advertising ID (see above) or contact that partner directly.

5. Children

Gravity Garden is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. We do not sell personal information in exchange for money. Sharing the advertising ID with ad networks for personalised ads may count as “sharing” under some U.S. state laws; you can opt out using the device settings in section 4.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
